1. Professional Reference & Clinical Decision Support Standards

The Clinical & Medical Differential Diagnosis Desk Reference PWA is an independent clinical decision-support and educational reference tool designed for licensed mental health professionals, physicians, clinical nurse specialists, medical trainees, and multidisciplinary researchers.

Clinician Responsibility: Diagnostic formulations, laboratory order selection, emergency transfer directives, and treatment decisions remain the exclusive professional responsibility of the attending licensed clinician. This reference tool is designed to assist in counteracting diagnostic overshadowing by systematically identifying interdisciplinary medical mimics, but does not substitute for formal medical consultation, in-person physical examination, or emergency department clearance protocols.

2. Zero-PHI Privacy Charter & HIPAA Compliance Declaration

🔒 100% In-Browser Volatile Memory Execution

This web application operates under a strict Zero-PHI (Protected Health Information) architecture. No data entered into this portal is ever transmitted across the internet, logged on remote servers, or stored in persistent browser cookies.

  • No Tracking or Analytics: There are no Google Analytics, third-party marketing tags, pixels, or trackers of any kind.
  • Local RAM Isolation: Patient identifiers, symptoms, and clinician notes entered into the referral forms exist exclusively in your computer or mobile device's temporary memory (RAM) and evaporate completely upon closing or refreshing the tab.
  • Offline-First Storage: Diagnostic databases and monographs are cached locally via modern Service Worker technology, allowing complete functionality even in airplane mode or secure healthcare facilities with disconnected Wi-Fi.
  • Compliance: Designed in accordance with HIPAA Security and Privacy Rules (§164.312) regarding technical safeguards for electronic health information transmission.
