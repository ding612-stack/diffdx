Professional Reference Standards

The Clinical & Medical Differential Diagnosis Desk Reference PWA is an independent clinical decision-support and educational reference tool designed for licensed mental health professionals, physicians, medical trainees, and clinical researchers.

Clinician Responsibility: Diagnostic formulations, laboratory order selection, emergency transfer directives, and treatment decisions remain the exclusive professional responsibility of the attending licensed clinician. This reference tool is designed to assist in counteracting diagnostic overshadowing by systematically identifying interdisciplinary medical mimics, but does not substitute for formal medical consultation, physical examination, or emergency department clearance protocols.

Zero-PHI Privacy Guarantee: All patient data entered into the referral forms executes 100% locally within volatile browser RAM and is never stored, tracked, or transmitted across any network.
